AI Island Support

Last updated: September 14, 2026

AI Island shows supported AI-service usage limits and reset times in the app, Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen Live Activities, and Dynamic Island on supported iPhones.

Need help?

Email AI Island Support or open a support request on GitHub Issues. Please do not include passwords, session tokens, purchase receipts, or other private credentials.

Connect a provider account

Open AI Island, choose a supported provider, and follow its connection instructions. Provider credentials are stored in the iPhone Keychain and sent directly to that provider. AI Island does not operate an account server.

Live Activity or Dynamic Island is not appearing

  • Confirm Live Activities are allowed for AI Island in iOS Settings.
  • Open AI Island and refresh the connected account.
  • Dynamic Island requires a supported iPhone; the Lock Screen Live Activity remains available on other supported iPhones.
  • iOS controls background refresh timing, so updates may not occur at an exact interval.

Restore a Pro purchase

Open Settings → AI Island Pro and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If plans are temporarily unavailable, Restore purchases remains accessible.

Cancel or manage a subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions, select AI Island, and choose the available management or cancellation option. A Lifetime purchase does not renew.

Delete local AI Island data

Disconnect each provider account inside AI Island to remove its saved credentials and local usage snapshot. You can also delete the app to remove its app data. If you contact support, never send provider session tokens or passwords.
