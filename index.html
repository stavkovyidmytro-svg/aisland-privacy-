AI Island — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

AI Island is an iPhone app that shows your usage of AI coding tools (Claude Code, ChatGPT/Codex, Perplexity, GitHub Copilot) in the Dynamic Island, Lock Screen, and widgets. This page describes, plainly, what the app does with your data.

The short version

  • AI Island has no account server. We do not operate any backend that receives, stores, or processes your data.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with anyone, because we never receive it in the first place.
  • The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind.

What the app does with your data

When you connect an AI provider account, the app talks directly to that provider (Claude, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Perplexity, or GitHub) to read your usage figures. That connection is between your iPhone and the provider you chose — it does not pass through us.

  • Credentials (session tokens or API keys you provide) are stored in the iPhone Keychain, protected by the operating system, and are only ever sent to the provider you connected them to.
  • Usage figures (cost, token counts, reset times) are kept in a local App Group container shared only between the app and its widget/Live Activity extension, so they can be displayed on your Lock Screen and Home Screen. This data never leaves your device.

Optional: Mac bridge (Pro feature)

Pro subscribers can optionally run a small background script on their own Mac that summarizes local Claude Code logs and writes a reduced usage summary (cost, token counts — never prompts or conversation content) to a private GitHub Gist that you create and own. The iPhone app then reads that gist using a token you provide. We never see this data; it travels directly between your Mac, GitHub, and your iPhone.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell or share data with data brokers — we have none to sell.
  • We don't require an account with us to use the app.

Third-party services you connect

Your use of Claude, ChatGPT/OpenAI, Perplexity, or GitHub through this app is subject to each provider's own privacy policy and terms, since your data is read directly from their services:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Perplexity Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

Children's privacy

AI Island is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age — the app has no server to collect it with.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Since the app collects nothing on our end, changes here would only ever describe new optional features, not a change in how existing data is handled.
